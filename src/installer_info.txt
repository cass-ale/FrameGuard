FrameGuard Desktop v0.8.1
Video QA for XR Deliverables


WHAT THIS INSTALLS

  FrameGuard Desktop
    Checks video files against your delivery specification and reports what
    passed, what failed, and why. A whole folder can be scanned in one pass.
    Failures sort first, with critical issues (corruption, unreadable files)
    ranked above minor ones.

  FFmpeg and FFprobe
    The video analysis engine. Bundled with this installer; no separate
    download or setup is required.

  MediaInfo
    Adds encoder and container detail to reports.

  Delivery presets
    4K UHD 30/60, 1080p 30, and 5.7K VR/360 profiles to start from. Your own
    presets can be saved and shared with your team as .fg files.


WHAT'S NEW IN v0.8.1

  More accurate deep scans
    - Separate silences, freezes and black sequences are each reported on
      their own instead of being merged into one long false event.
    - An entirely black file, or one that opens or ends on long black, is now
      reported; short fades are still ignored.
    - File names containing words such as "corrupt" no longer fail Decode
      Integrity, and Matroska/WebM files no longer raise spurious warnings.
    - Preset values are type-checked before they reach FFmpeg.

  Smoother updates and a calmer interface
    - In-app updates show progress, close FrameGuard for the installer and
      reopen it when the update finishes.
    - Tray actions and quitting no longer crash; the splash screen no longer
      freezes during startup.
    - A seamless sidebar, real chevrons, ticks and radio dots, and tighter,
      aligned spacing throughout.


BEFORE YOU INSTALL

  Requirements
    Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
    4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended for large batches
    500 MB free disk space

  If FrameGuard is running when Setup starts, Setup will close it
  automatically.


PRIVACY

  All processing happens on this computer. FrameGuard does not upload,
  transmit, or share your video files or scan results.
