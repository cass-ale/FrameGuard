FrameGuard Desktop v0.9.2
Video QA for XR Deliverables


WHAT THIS INSTALLS

  FrameGuard Desktop
    Checks video files against your delivery specification and reports what
    passed, what failed, and why. A whole folder can be scanned in one pass.
    Failures sort first, with critical issues (corruption, unreadable files)
    ranked above minor ones.

  FFmpeg and FFprobe
    The video analysis engine. Bundled with this installer; no separate
    download or setup is required.

  MediaInfo
    Adds encoder and container detail to reports.

  Delivery presets
    4K UHD 30/60, 1080p 30, and 5.7K VR/360 profiles to start from. Your own
    presets can be saved and shared with your team as .fg files.


WHAT'S NEW IN v0.9.2

  Fixes from an independent review
    - Flash screening judges every region of the screen, down to fine
      patterns; HDR video shows "Not verified" instead of skipping.
    - Interlacing and 3:2 pulldown are found in real camera footage.
    - Picture levels follow EBU R 103 exactly, at full resolution.
    - Truncated files, quiet room tone, hum, phase and ambisonic peaks are
      judged correctly; anything FFmpeg could not measure says so.

  Stricter where it matters
    - Disguised containers fail, and an unread file structure is never a
      pass. Presets from 0.9.1 load, with every adjustment shown.

  Also in v0.9.1
    - Broadcast-grade loudness, picture and conformance checks, and a
      native Windows window.


BEFORE YOU INSTALL

  Requirements
    Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
    4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended for large batches
    500 MB free disk space

  If FrameGuard is running when Setup starts, Setup will close it
  automatically.


PRIVACY

  All processing happens on this computer. FrameGuard does not upload,
  transmit, or share your video files or scan results.
