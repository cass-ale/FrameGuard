FrameGuard Desktop v0.6.0
Video QA for XR Deliverables


WHAT THIS INSTALLS

  FrameGuard Desktop
    Checks video files against your delivery specification and reports what
    passed, what failed, and why. A whole folder can be scanned in one pass.
    Failures sort first, with critical issues (corruption, unreadable files)
    ranked above minor ones.

  FFmpeg and FFprobe
    The video analysis engine. Bundled with this installer; no separate
    download or setup is required.

  MediaInfo
    Adds encoder and container detail to reports.

  Delivery presets
    4K UHD 30/60, 1080p 30, and 5.7K VR/360 profiles to start from. Your own
    presets can be saved and shared with your team as .fg files.


WHAT'S NEW IN v0.6.0

  Release pipeline repair
    - The macOS build could not finish: the FFmpeg and FFprobe downloads
      pointed at a retired source, so the build failed at the last step.
      The tools are now downloaded per architecture (Apple Silicon and
      Intel separately), pinned to a specific build, and verified against
      the publisher's SHA-256 checksums before they are bundled.
    - MediaInfo silently went missing from macOS builds: unpacking its
      installer package failed without a trace. It is now unpacked
      reliably, and failures are reported instead of swallowed.
    - Both builders now stop immediately, with the real cause, when a
      required tool or a test fails, instead of warning and failing later
      with an unrelated message.
    - The Windows test gate could stall forever on a startup dialog that
      automated runs could never dismiss. The check no longer opens
      dialogs when the app runs without a display, and every test now
      has a time limit.
    - App identity moved to the publisher's own: the macOS bundle ID is
      now com.caprilaria.frameguard, and the organization domain is
      caprilaria.com. On macOS, preferences and preset-file ownership
      start fresh under the new identity.
    - Bundled MediaInfo updated to 26.10.
    - No changes to scanning behaviour.


BEFORE YOU INSTALL

  Requirements
    Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
    4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended for large batches
    500 MB free disk space

  If FrameGuard is running when Setup starts, Setup will close it
  automatically.


PRIVACY

  All processing happens on this computer. FrameGuard does not upload,
  transmit, or share your video files or scan results.
