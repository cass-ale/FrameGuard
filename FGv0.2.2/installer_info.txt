FrameGuard Desktop v0.5.5
Video QA for XR Deliverables


WHAT THIS INSTALLS

  FrameGuard Desktop
    Checks video files against your delivery specification and reports what
    passed, what failed, and why. A whole folder can be scanned in one pass.
    Failures sort first, with critical issues (corruption, unreadable files)
    ranked above minor ones.

  FFmpeg and FFprobe
    The video analysis engine. Bundled with this installer; no separate
    download or setup is required.

  MediaInfo
    Adds encoder and container detail to reports.

  Delivery presets
    4K UHD 30/60, 1080p 30, and 5.7K VR/360 profiles to start from. Your own
    presets can be saved and shared with your team as .fg files.


WHAT'S NEW IN v0.5.5

  Hardening
    - The scan page verdict clears with the results, and transient notes
      dismiss themselves instead of lingering; failures stay until you act
      on them.
    - Strict Mode now takes effect: with it on, warnings escalate to FAIL.
    - "Open in VLC" re-detects installed players on each use, falls back
      when a launch fails, and reports when nothing could be launched
      instead of failing silently.
    - The startup splash tracks progress while the window builds, and
      startup no longer waits on the FFmpeg check before the window paints.

  If you signed off footage using v0.5.2 or earlier, it is worth
  re-scanning: the deep-scan checks in those versions could report "no
  issues found" for files that had them.


BEFORE YOU INSTALL

  Requirements
    Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
    4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended for large batches
    500 MB free disk space

  If FrameGuard is running when Setup starts, Setup will close it
  automatically.


PRIVACY

  All processing happens on this computer. FrameGuard does not upload,
  transmit, or share your video files or scan results.
