FrameGuard Desktop v0.5.4
Video QA for XR Deliverables


WHAT THIS INSTALLS

  FrameGuard Desktop
    Checks video files against your delivery specification and reports what
    passed, what failed, and why. Scan a whole folder in one pass.
    Failures sort first with critical issues (corruption, unreadable files)
    ranked above minor ones.

  FFmpeg and FFprobe
    The video analysis engine. Bundled with this installer - no separate
    download or setup required.

  MediaInfo
    Adds encoder and container detail to reports.

  Delivery presets
    4K UHD 30/60, 1080p 30, and 5.7K VR/360 profiles to start from. You can
    save your own and share them with your team as .fg files.


WHAT'S NEW IN v0.5.4

  Stability
    - Deep scans no longer crash on large or long files: scan parallelism
      and memory use are bounded, cancellation stops promptly, and missing
      tools report a clear message instead of an "Internal Error".
    - Scan verdict banner clears on new files, navigation, and errors, and
      clean results dismiss themselves; failures stay until you act on them.
    - Results are severity-weighted: a corrupted file ranks above one with
      only an off-spec frame rate.

  This release corrects several issues that produced misleading scan results
  in earlier versions (v0.5.2):

    - Deep scan (black frames, freeze frames, silence) reported "no issues
      found" on every file. These checks now work.
    - Settings and presets had no effect on scans.
    - Reports covering more than one file failed to generate.
    - Files slightly under the bitrate target were reported as an internal
      error rather than a bitrate warning.

  If you signed off footage using an earlier version, it is worth re-scanning.


BEFORE YOU INSTALL

  Requirements
    Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
    4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended for large batches
    500 MB free disk space

  Close FrameGuard if it is currently running. Setup will prompt you if it
  detects a running copy.


PRIVACY

  All processing happens on this computer. FrameGuard does not upload,
  transmit, or share your video files or scan results.
